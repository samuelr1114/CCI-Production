Service-Day Checklist

Sound Setup

Follow this checklist for normal setup. If something is unclear, use the Connection & Equipment Reference rather than guessing.

1. Bring In Equipment

Bring the sound equipment/rack into position and prepare the stage for connections.

2. Connect Digital Audio — AES50

WING AES50 A → S16 #1 AES50 A
S16 #1 AES50 B → S16 #2 AES50 A
Verify GREEN at WING AES50 A, S16 #1 A, S16 #1 B, and S16 #2 A.
WING A 🟢 → 🟢 A [ S16 #1 ] B 🟢 → 🟢 A [ S16 #2 ]

3. Connect Stage Audio

Piano & Bass
Piano / Bass / Drum IEMs
Singer IEMs
PA System

4. Connect WING / Playback

iPad Pro → WING square USB port for Playback.
White Ethernet cable → either WING network port → router.
Open Playback and verify connection.

5. Power Up

Power distributor by projector FIRST.
WING + S16 rack next — either order.
Left speakers + monitors FIRST.
Right speaker LAST.
Power-down is the reverse: right speaker first → left speakers + monitors → WING + S16 → distributor last.

6. Line Check & Soundcheck

Singer microphones
IEMs
Piano
Bass
Spotify tracks
Playback tracks
Confirm audio is reaching the ATEM Mini for the stream.
