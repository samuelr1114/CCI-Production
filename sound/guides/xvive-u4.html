Channel check

The transmitter and its receiver must be on the same channel.

Two IEM systems should not use the same channel.

Power on the transmitter and receiver and verify the unit shows green / active.

Service setup

Get the 3 Xvive sets from the charging chest and connect the transmitters to the XLR cables routed to the top of the rack.
