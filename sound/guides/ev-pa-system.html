Important: Never connect the monitor chain to the subwoofer chain. The right monitor and right sub are the ends of their separate chains.
Main speakers — EV EKX-15P

Use INPUT 1 for the feed.

Subwoofers — EV ELX118P

Feed enters INPUT. Daisy chain leaves LINK OUTPUT.

Monitors — EV ELX200

Feed enters INPUT 1. Daisy chain leaves OUTPUT.

Left-side cable bundle

Monitor + Main Left + Sub cables run together to the left side. Yellow = monitors; green = mains/sub.
