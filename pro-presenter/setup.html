Service-Day Checklist

ProPresenter Setup

Connect and verify the Mac mini display outputs before rehearsal. The normal desk monitor HDMI stays connected.

1. Booth Monitor

Verify the normal monitor is connected directly to the Mac mini's built-in HDMI port.
Leave this HDMI connected. This is the normal ProPresenter operator monitor.

2. Connect Stream Output

Connect the HDMI cable for the ATEM Mini stream output to the HDMI → USB-C adapter.
Connect the adapter to the Mac mini.

3. Connect Confidence TV

Connect the HDMI cable for the confidence TV in the back to the HDMI → USB-A adapter.
Connect the adapter to the Mac mini.

4. Connect Main Projector

Connect the projector output from the Mac mini USB-C connection to the Blackmagic converter.
Verify SDI is connected from the booth-side Blackmagic converter to the projector-side Blackmagic converter.
At the projector, verify the second Blackmagic converter changes the SDI signal back to HDMI and feeds the projector.

5. Verify ProPresenter Screen Assignments

Stream is assigned to the ATEM Mini stream output.
Stage is assigned to the confidence TV in the back.
Audience or Main is assigned to the projector.

6. Verify All Displays

Booth / operator monitor is visible.
Stream output is reaching the ATEM Mini.
Stage output is visible on the confidence TV.
Audience / Main output is visible on the projector.
